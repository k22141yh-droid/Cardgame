========================================================
  RekindledFX - Lava
  Procedural lava flow shader + rising embers for Unity URP
========================================================

Thank you for purchasing RekindledFX Lava!

--------------------------------------------------------
QUICK START (60 seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the demo scene:
   Assets/RekindledFX/Elemental/Demo/LavaDemo.unity
2. Press Play (or just look at the Scene view - it previews live).
3. To use it in your own scene:
   - Drag the "LavaPool" prefab from
     Assets/RekindledFX/Elemental/Prefabs/ into your scene, OR
   - Apply the "Lava" material (Assets/RekindledFX/Elemental/Materials/)
     to any mesh, then add the LavaEmbers component / prefab on top.

--------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------
- Universal Render Pipeline (URP). Developed and tested on Unity 6.
- Post-processing Bloom is recommended (the lava glow is HDR emissive
  and pops with Bloom enabled).
- No Built-in or HDRP support in this version (URP only).

--------------------------------------------------------
WHAT'S INCLUDED
--------------------------------------------------------
- LavaFlow surface shader (RekindledFX/Elemental/LavaFlow)
- ElementalParticle shader (used by the embers)
- LavaEmbers component (rising ember particles)
- Ready material presets (Lava, LavaCurrent)
- Example prefabs and a demo scene

--------------------------------------------------------
ZERO DEPENDENCIES
--------------------------------------------------------
Everything is procedural - no external textures, no other packages
required. Just import and use.
